Privacy Policy

Nails Art Salon Game · Last updated: 24 October 2026

Nails Art Salon Game ("the app") is a nail-design game published by Zeeshan Farooq. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

The app works fully offline. It does not ask you to create an account and does not use the internet, camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (your level, coins, the items you have unlocked, the custom polish colours you mix and your sound setting) is saved only on your own device so you can continue playing later. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Third-party services

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services, and no in-app purchases.

Children

The app is suitable for children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If this ever changes (for example if online features or ads are added), this policy will be updated first and will comply with applicable children's privacy laws.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
